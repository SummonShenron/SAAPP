2 · GraphRAG retrieval

Three ways to search, picked by plain rules

No extra model call: the question's wording chooses the strategy.

Vector

Used foreverything else

Howembed the question, take the nearest chunks by meaning

Lexical

Triggered byrecent, latest, when, date, current, last

Howpull a wide candidate pool, then re-rank by keyword scoring (TF‑IDF)

Hybrid

Triggered bycompare, versus, relationship, how does, why did, or a long question

Howrun both, then fuse the two rankings (reciprocal rank fusion)

Checked in this orderkeyword words first, then comparison words or a long question, otherwise plain vector search
Always inside the scope filterThe strategy changes how chunks are ranked, never which libraries are read.
