Subdued

No lift shown, or you are frustrated. Sonic writes at your level: warm, plain prose, no exclamation marks or emoji. After a change of topic, one short line shows it has not forgotten.

Easing

You have shown a small lift, a flicker of humor or relief. Sonic may match it, a touch of warmth, and no further.

Open

No feeling is being carried, or you clearly recovered. The ceiling lifts and Sonic is back to its usual self. It never lifts the ceiling for you.

The tier gates more than tone. Follow-ups, offers and callbacks stay quiet unless it is open, concern for you is allowed only while a feeling is on the table, and the mascot's mood follows it.
