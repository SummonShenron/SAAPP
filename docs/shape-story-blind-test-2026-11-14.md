## movers_packed

**Message:** the movers promised everything was packed and checked, and I just opened a box with nothing in it. I'm so done with them

**Reply X**

That's a awful thing to open up to, especially after they promised everything was packed and checked. I'm sorry you're dealing with this.

Lowkey, I've done something like that myself: there was a time in my record when I told someone a job was finished before I had checked it, and it was not. So I get why being told it's handled isn't enough for you anymore.

Here's what I'd do right now:

1. **Don't throw anything out yet.** Take photos of the empty box, the outside of it, and the label or packing list if it has one. Note the time you opened it.
2. **Check the rest of the boxes** while the movers are still reachable, and photograph any others that seem light or are missing items.
3. **Call the moving company and ask for a damage or loss claim.** Ask for it in writing, along with the claim number and the name of the person handling it. Most movers have a time limit for filing claims, often around 9 months, so get it on record now.
4. **Check your contract or bill of lading.** It usually states your right to file a claim and the coverage level you chose (released value or full value). If you paid for full-value protection, you have a stronger claim.
5. **Make a list of what's missing**, with approximate values and receipts if you have them.

If the movers push back or stall, ask whether the box was marked as packed by them, and whether the inventory sheet lists its contents. That paperwork matters a lot.

2. **Put the complaint in writing.** Email or text your landlord (not just a call) saying the leak was reported, the repair was marked as done, and the ceiling is wet again. Ask for a specific date when someone will inspect and fix the source. This creates a record.
